# Ho So De Cu Nop Oxfam

## 1) Ten du an
Quang Trung Cultural Eco Zone - Ha Giang Smart Cultural Landscape

## 2) Van de xa hoi can giai quyet
Khu vuc Ha Giang 1, Ha Giang 2 va To 5 phuong Quang Trung co nhieu tai nguyen van hoa, nong nghiep, cho truyen thong, nhung:
- Gia tri van hoa dia phuong chua duoc so hoa va ke chuyen he thong.
- Truc tiep thuong mai cua ho kinh doanh nho con manh mun.
- Hoat dong bao ton canh quan va bao ve moi truong chua co co che tuong tac lien tuc voi nguoi dan va du khach.

## 3) Giai phap
Nen tang web tao he sinh thai trai nghiem so, ket noi:
- Diem den van hoa - lich su - cho truyen thong qua quet QR.
- Mang luoi cua hang dia phuong va check-in trai nghiem.
- Co che diem thuong xanh, huy hieu, bang xep hang cong dong.
- Cong cu bao cao hien trang canh quan/moi truong tu cong dong.

## 4) Doi tuong huong loi
- Ho kinh doanh nho, hop tac xa, nhom nghe nhan.
- Thanh nien, phu nu, nguoi dan To 5 phuong Quang Trung.
- Du khach trong nuoc va quoc te.

## 5) Tac dong du kien
- Tang luu luong tham gia trai nghiem va tieu dung tai diem den dia phuong.
- Tang doanh thu cua hang lien ket nho QR check-in va uu dai.
- Tang nhan thuc bao ton van hoa va bao ve moi truong qua hoat dong tham gia cong dong.
- Tao du lieu minh chung (luot quet, diem, bao cao) de quan tri chuong trinh phat trien ben vung.

## 6) Tinh nang da trien khai (MVP)
- QR Storytelling cho cac diem van hoa/sinh thai.
- Store Experience Network cho cua hang dia phuong.
- Store check-in va cong diem trai nghiem.
- Community Participation: gui bao cao bao ton.
- Landscape Conservation Tracking: tong hop bao cao.
- Offline demo mode khi backend chua san sang.

## 7) Cac diem da dua vao he thong
- Lang van hoa Lung Cam
- Nong trai che Shan Tuyet Hoang Su Phi
- Diem lich su Chua Trien Ha Giang 1
- Cho Lon Ha Giang 1
- Cho Phien Ha Giang 2
- Khong gian lich su cong dong Ha Giang 2
- Quang Trung Cultural Eco Zone - To 5

## 8) Thanh phan doi moi
- Chuyen doi tu diem den tinh sang he thong trai nghiem co thuong hieu, co du lieu, co tuong tac.
- Lien thong van hoa - thuong mai dia phuong - moi truong trong mot nen tang duy nhat.
- Mo hinh co the nhan rong cho cac cum phuong/xa khac.

## 9) Ke hoach 3-6 thang
- Thang 1-2: van hanh pilot tai To 5 Quang Trung + Ha Giang 1/2.
- Thang 3-4: bo sung them cua hang/diem den va chuan hoa quy trinh van hanh.
- Thang 5-6: mo rong lien phuong, bo sung dashboard quan tri va bao cao tac dong.

## 10) Nhu cau dong hanh tu Oxfam
- Tu van khung tac dong xa hoi va giam sat danh gia.
- Ho tro ket noi mang luoi cong dong, doi tac dia phuong.
- Dong hanh nang cao nang luc van hanh so cho nhom thuc thi.

## 11) Trang thai san sang
- Frontend da build thanh cong.
- Da co route eco-system va cac module trai nghiem.
- Co the demo ngay tren web de minh hoa luong su dung.
